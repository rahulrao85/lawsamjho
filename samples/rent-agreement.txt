RENT AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is made at Mumbai on 01-Apr-2026 between Mr. Ramesh Vasant
Kulkarni, residing at Flat 302, Shanti Residency, Andheri West, Mumbai 400058
(hereinafter called "the Licensor") of the ONE PART and Ms. Priya Sundaram,
residing at 14-B, Rose Villa, Bandra East, Mumbai 400051 (hereinafter called
"the Licensee") of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 704, Shanti Residency,
Andheri West, Mumbai 400058 admeasuring 780 sq. ft. (hereinafter called "the
Premises") and has agreed to grant the Licensee a licence to occupy the
Premises on the terms recorded below.

1. TERM. The licence shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing on
01-Apr-2026 and ending on 28-Feb-2027, and shall not be extended except by a
fresh written agreement signed by both parties.

2. RENT. The Licensee shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 45,000 (Rupees Forty Five
Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the seventh day of each
calendar month by bank transfer to the Licensor's account.

3. ESCALATION. The Licensor may increase the monthly rent by up to ten percent
(10%) upon any renewal of this agreement, and the Licensee shall have no right
to object to such increase.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable security
deposit of Rs. 90,000 (Rupees Ninety Thousand only). The deposit shall be
refunded within ninety (90) days of the Licensee vacating the Premises, after
deduction of any amounts due.

5. DEDUCTIONS. The Licensor may deduct from the security deposit the cost of
painting the Premises, whether or not such painting is required, at the rate of
Rs. 15,000.

6. MAINTENANCE. The Licensee shall pay maintenance charges to the society
directly, together with electricity and water charges for the Premises based on
actual consumption.

7. USE. The Premises shall be used for residential purposes only. The Licensee
shall not carry on any business, trade or profession from the Premises.

8. SUBLETTING. The Licensee shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of
the Premises or any part of it without the prior written consent of the
Licensor.

9. ENTRY. The Licensor or his authorised representative may enter the Premises
at any reasonable time for inspection, and the Licensee shall permit such entry.

10. LOCK-IN. The Licensee shall not terminate this agreement before the expiry
of six (6) months from the commencement date. If the Licensee vacates during
the lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay the rent for the unexpired portion of
the lock-in period.

11. NOTICE. After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement
by giving one (1) month's written notice. Notice given by the Licensee shall be
sent to the Licensor by registered post only.

12. FURNISHING. The Licensor shall provide the furniture and fixtures listed in
the Schedule. The Licensee shall return the same in good condition at the time
of vacating, subject to normal wear and tear.

13. REPAIRS. The Licensee shall keep the Premises in good repair and shall bear
the cost of any repairs required to the internal fittings and fixtures.

14. UTILITIES. The Licensee shall pay all charges for electricity, water and
internet consumed at the Premises from the commencement date until the date of
vacating.

15. INDEMNITY. The Licensee shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Licensor
against all claims, losses and damages arising from the Licensee's use or
occupation of the Premises.

16. DEFAULT. If the Licensee fails to pay rent for two consecutive months, the
Licensor shall be entitled to terminate this agreement with immediate effect and
to re-enter the Premises without any notice or court order.

17. DISPUTES. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement
shall be referred to the sole arbitration of an arbitrator to be appointed by
the Licensor, and the decision of such arbitrator shall be final and binding.

18. JURISDICTION. This agreement shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of India.

above written.
